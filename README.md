
# ☕ Bright Coffee Sales Analysis

A data analytics case study conducted for Bright Coffee Shop, undertaken as a Junior Data Analyst to help the newly appointed CEO grow revenue and improve product performance through data-driven insight.

# 📋 Overview

Bright Coffee Shop's new CEO set a mission to grow company revenue and improve product performance. As the Junior analyst on this project, the goal was to extract actionable insights from historical transactional data and present findings that support the CEO's decision-making — covering an end-to-end workflow from planning and architecture, through data processing and analysis, to a final stakeholder presentation.Using analytics, SQL, and data visualization skills, the project set out to help Bright Coffee Shop understand:

Which products generate the most revenue?

What time of day the store performs best?

Sales trends across products and time intervals?

Recommendations for improving sales performance?


# 🗂️ Project Stages
## Stage 1 — Project Planning (Miro and Garntt Chart)

The first stage involved understanding the business problem, defining the analytical questions, planning the workflow, and deciding how the final results would be communicated.

## Stage 2 - Data Processing (Databricks / SQL)

Converted the raw source data to CSV and loaded it for processing

Cleaned and transformed the dataset by  standardizing the  unit_price 

Time-bucket groupings (Transaction_time_bucket in 3-hour intervals 

Simplified Time_bucket: Morning/Afternoon/Evening/Night/Closing Hours)

Used SQL to group and aggregate transactions by product type, category, store location, and time bucket


## Stage 3 - Data Analysis (Data Studio/ Google Sheets / Power BI /Databricks)

Exported the processed dataset and built pivot tables and dashboards covering total revenue per product type, peak time intervals, quantity sold by category, and best-selling product details
Built visual charts and graphs to surface trends clearly for a non-technical audience.

# 💡 Recommendations

Morning is the strongest sales period.

Bright Coffee Shop should:

Ensure sufficient staff and stock during morning periods.
Promote breakfast combinations like coffee/tea with bakery
Introduce morning-specific promotions for loyal customers.

On underperforming Productsthey should not automatically be removed.
Instead, management should investigate:

Customer demands,
Pricing on each product,
Product visibility,and 
time-of-day performance.

Again Bright Coffee should Use Slow Periods for Marketing beause closing hours and weaker sales periods provide opportunities for targeted promotions.
The business could introduce:

>Time-limited discounts

>Buy-one coffee and -get-one free

>Loyalty rewards

>Happy-hour style promotions
